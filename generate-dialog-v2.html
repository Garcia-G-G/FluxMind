Gamma-inspired multi-step flow. Each step focuses on ONE decision. Step 1: what to focus on. Step 2: choose a visual style from LARGE colored preview cards (the options ARE the visuals). Step 3: fine-tune. Progressive disclosure keeps each screen simple.

Single modal but the options are ILLUSTRATIONS showing what each choice produces. Orientation is shown as mini infographic silhouettes. Style tiles have colored backgrounds. 3 — "Fullscreen Takeover"
Gamma-style: takes over the screen. The only thing you see are 6 LARGE colored style cards — they ARE the entire screen. Style is the hero decision. All other settings (orientation, detail, accent, language) are compressed into a compact bottom bar as secondary controls. The style choice IS the UI. Everything else is a footnote.
