B — "Side Panel"
Not a modal — a right panel that slides in (like ElevenLabs). Settings are in collapsible accordion sections so the panel stays compact. Segmented controls instead of tile grids. Sticky generate bar at the bottom. The content behind stays visible and interactive. Best for frequent generation workflows.

C — "Two-Column / Vercel"
Clean modal with a two-column layout. Left side has settings using pill/chip selectors. Right side shows a live summary of your selections — what orientation, style, how many sources, etc. White "Generate" button (Vercel style). The summary column gives instant feedback on what you've configured without cluttering the controls.
